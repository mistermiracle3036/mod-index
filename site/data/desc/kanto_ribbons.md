Permanent, per-Pokemon **ribbons** for gen1recomp -- Red, Blue, Yellow
and **Gold, Silver and Crystal** -- reimagined from the Generation III-IX
ribbon system for things these games actually simulate.

Ribbons live on the Pokemon itself, so they survive boxing, evolution and
trades, and they open straight from the status screen: press A past the
last page. On Gen 2 they sit as a fourth page, so left and right reach
them too.

*(Formerly "Kanto Ribbons" -- renamed with Gen 2 support. Same mod, same
saves, nothing to reinstall.)*

## Features

- **Thirty-eight ribbons**, earned several ways: story milestones, hard
  training, flawless battles, lucky DVs, Contest wins, and one very
  expensive shop. Eighteen are always available; twenty more are Contest
  ribbons, one per category per rank, with **Kanto Contests** installed.
- **Awarded automatically.** Nothing to toggle or claim. If a Pokemon
  qualifies, it already has the ribbon by the time you look.
- **Retroactive on existing saves.** Most ribbons are recomputed from your
  save on load, so a run started before installing catches up on its own.
  Where a save genuinely records nothing to work from, the mod says so in
  the log instead of guessing. Nothing ever revokes a ribbon already
  awarded, so updating is always safe.
- **Opens from the status screen** in every UI: STATS -> MOVES -> RIBBONS,
  all on the A button. Only ribbons a Pokemon *has* are listed, four at a
  time.
- **A shop.** The Meowth in Celadon Mansion 1F sells three ribbons at
  escalating, deliberately absurd prices, for whichever Pokemon is at the
  front of your party. Kanto games only for now.
- **Gen 2 extras.** Best Friends comes from native happiness, and Summit is
  earned by beating Red on Mt Silver.
- **One self-contained package.** No engine files to replace, no companion
  downloads.

Optional: recognises **Pokemon Snag** (the Snag Ribbon), **Kanto
Contests** (the twenty Contest ribbons) and Kanto Achievements when they
are installed. Contest wins are recorded on the Pokemon itself, so a
Pokemon that won before you installed this still gets its ribbon on the
next save load.
