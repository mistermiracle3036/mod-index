**Pokemon GOLD only.** This mod does not load on Red, Blue or Yellow — it
will simply be skipped on those games.

ROXIE came to JOHTO looking for a music scene and found temple bells, monks
and somebody's sleeping WOOPER. So she is starting a band out of spite, and
you are her manager.

> **ALPHA, and the band is half-assembled.** The questline runs from meeting
> ROXIE to recruiting the drummer, and then stops. Promotion, the gig and
> PIERS come later. It is playable and stable as far as it goes, and bug
> reports are the point of releasing it this early — please include the
> version from the load banner and which other mods were enabled.

## The band so far

**ROXIE** turns up near the VIOLET GYM once you have a badge, challenges
you, and appoints you as her manager whether you agreed or not.

**FEEDBACK**, the bassist, plays GOLDENROD's UNDERGROUND under a false name
and in a kimono. Find her, and bring three badges if you want the audition —
the battle introduction does the rest of the reveal for you.

**CASEY** has to be worked out rather than found. A drum audition in ECRUTEAK
gets interrupted by a JIGGLYPUFF, and three candidates each have their own
idea of a beat. Identify who actually kept time.

## Compatibility

Designed to sit alongside other mods. **No vanilla maps, NPCs, scripts or
trainers are modified** — ROXIE and the rest are runtime objects and are
never written into your save, so uninstalling leaves nothing behind.

**Quest System** is supported if you have it, and entirely optional. Without
it the questline reads the same through dialogue.

## Known gaps

Promotion, the gig and PIERS are not in yet, and the story ends once the
drummer joins. Any beats past that point are unwritten rather than broken.
