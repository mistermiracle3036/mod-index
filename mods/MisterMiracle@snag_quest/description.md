Throw a ball at another trainer's Pokemon and take it.

Pokemon Snag adds the **Snag Ball** to gen1recomp (Red / Blue / Yellow): a
real, reusable ball that works in *trainer* battles, so any Pokemon an
opponent sends out is a Pokemon you can walk away with.

Jessie recruits you into the business in Viridian City, and a network of
black-market fences across Kanto pays you in Snag Balls for the goods --
so the better your thefts, the more you can steal.

## Features

- **The intro quest.** After the Pokedex, find Jessie in Viridian City.
  She sets you up with your first Snag Ball and a target: a Picnicker with
  a very special Meowth.
- **The Snag Ball.** Throw it at an opponent's Pokemon and it is yours.
  Snagged Pokemon are marked permanently and keep a record of who you took
  them from and at what level.
- **Keep fighting.** By default the battle continues after a successful
  snag. Switchable in options.
- **Fences.** Certain NPCs quietly buy snagged Pokemon, paying 1-5 Snag
  Balls by level, rarity and origin. VIP targets fetch top price.
- **Choose your supply.** One setting decides where new Snag Balls come
  from: marts, fences, or both.

## Requires

**Quest System** must be installed and enabled.

Optional: pairs with **Pokeball Colors** (the Snag Ball gets a
Master-Ball-tier throw) and shiny-Pokemon mods.
