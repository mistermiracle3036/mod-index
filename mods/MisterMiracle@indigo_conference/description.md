**Pokemon GOLD only.** This mod does not load on Red, Blue or Yellow — it
will simply be skipped on those games.

Upstairs in every Pokemon Center there's a battle room nobody uses — the
link-battle room single player never sees. The Colosseum circuit fills it.
Climb the stairs in VIOLET and you've walked into the Violet Qualifier; do it
in BLACKTHORN and it's the Masters. Four rounds, one challenger each, a
different field every time, ending at the Indigo Plateau Conference itself.

**No new maps, no new warps, no new buildings.** Which event you get is
decided entirely by whose stairs you climbed.

## The five events

| Event | Climb the stairs in | To enter |
|---|---|---|
| Violet Qualifier | Violet City | the Zephyr Badge |
| Goldenrod Open | Goldenrod City | 3 Johto badges |
| Ecruteak Invitational | Ecruteak City | 4 Johto badges |
| Blackthorn Masters | Blackthorn City | 8 Johto badges |
| Indigo Plateau Conference | Indigo Plateau | beat the Elite Four |

Only **Johto** badges count. The host tells you what you're missing, and
being turned away costs nothing — a tournament running somewhere else is
left alone.

## Difficulty is fixed, not scaled to you

Each event is anchored to **that town's own gym leader** — a step above the
gym, climbing again with every round. So the Violet Qualifier stays an
early-game bracket and the Blackthorn Masters stays a hard one, whatever
level you turn up at. Winning means something because the bar doesn't move.

## Losing, and going again

A loss doesn't black you out: you don't lose money, you aren't sent back to a
Pokemon Center, and your team is patched up where it stands. **PP is not
restored**, so a fresh run is never free.

It's **repeatable** — take a title and you can enter again, and the announcer
draws a new card from the pool. A run in progress travels with your save, so
loading an older save rewinds the tournament along with it.

## Who you fight

39 guest challengers across four tiers, each fighting under their own name.
If you want to know who's in the pool or exactly how the levels are decided,
the repo's **FAQ and spoiler guide** keeps every answer collapsed so you only
reveal what you want to.

## Compatibility

**No vanilla maps, NPCs, scripts or trainers are modified.** Designed to sit
alongside other mods.
